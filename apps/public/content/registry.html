This registry is an evidence ledger, not a proclamation of final identity. “Tribal Nations” is retained in the requested title; within the entries, more precise terms - kingdom, federation, city-state, town, chiefdom, ritual polity, and self-governing community - are preferred.

NON-OVERLAP RULE

Section 1 contains named polities and explicitly qualified named candidates, including disputed or unlocated names. The twelve U-series entries in Section 2 are unresolved-origin research categories, not admitted unnamed polities. A separate unnamed-polity dossier requires positive evidence of a particular collective or political organization. Named and unnamed entity records must never overlap.

Section 3 — Classification controls

Class Admission rule
POLITY A dated self-governing political body: kingdom, federation, city-state, town polity, chiefdom, council republic, ritual polity, or other evidenced form.
PEOPLE An ethnolinguistic or cultural identity. It may span many polities and may remain a nation in a cultural sense without being one historical state.
PLACE A town, river, island, coast, ecological region, market, or port. A named place is not automatically a polity.
TRADE LABEL A commercial or colonial classification applied at sale, embarkation, or destination. It may aggregate unrelated origins.
PERSON An individual’s attributed self-name, imposed name, family, place, language, route, and record chain. A person can evidence a nation without representing everyone in it.
